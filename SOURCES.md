## Calculation rules

- Air freight volumetric weight: cm³ / 6000, chargeable weight rounded up to 0.5 kg. Sources: iata-tact-asean
- Sea freight (LCL) W/M: 1 m³ = 1000 kg, the larger value counts. Sources: wm-cogoport
- Road freight: 1 m³ = 333 kg, the larger value counts, rounded up to 1 kg. Sources: incodocs-ltl
