Who the routes are for

The service is meant for small and medium-sized companies that ship goods to the Gulf, China or India, or buy goods from there.

Exporters with customers in the Gulf

Whether a dealer, an end customer or a trade fair stand in the Emirates or Saudi Arabia: the goods go by air, sea or land to the recipient. With DDP your recipient pays nothing more on delivery; with DAP they pay the import charges themselves.

Buying in China, India and the Gulf

If you buy goods or components abroad, you need transport to Germany. In your request, name the pickup location at the supplier and the agreed delivery terms, for example EXW or FOB.

Beauty and cosmetics brands

Cosmetics and beauty products are among the typical goods on the route to the Gulf. If your shipment contains sprays, perfume or nail polish, please say so in your request.

Spare parts and trade fair goods

Urgent spare parts usually go by air, heavy or bulky parts by sea. Trade fair goods have a fixed date: name the fair, the set-up day and the stand number so that the mode of transport and lead time can be planned around it.

Services

Transport is organised by Trust Logistic FZCO in Dubai, in both directions: from Germany to the Gulf and back, and to and from China and India. Other routes are possible on request.

Air freight

For urgent and high-value shipments. From Germany to the Gulf typically 5–7 days door to door, depending on the shipment. For other routes, the transit time is stated in the offer.

Sea freight

For heavy or bulky shipments where transit time is not the main concern.

Sea-Air

A combination of sea and air legs. Transit time and cost lie between pure sea freight and pure air freight.

Road

Trust is also licensed as a land transport agency. Whether your shipment travels fully or partly by road is stated in the offer.

Delivery terms

Who pays for transport, customs duties and import taxes is set by the delivery terms in the sales contract, such as EXW, FOB, DAP or DDP. Name them in your request and the offer will follow them.

Incoterms at a glance

What is settled in the offer

Some points depend on the individual shipment. This site therefore does not promise them; you will find them in the offer.

  • Pickup of the goods at the sender, in Germany or abroad
  • Export clearance in the country of departure
  • Import clearance in the destination country, including Germany
  • Transport insurance and its terms
  • Goods that are not carried
  • Minimum weight per shipment

What does your shipment weigh for the freight bill?

Air freight is charged by weight or by volume, whichever is higher. An example: 4 cartons of 60 × 40 × 40 cm and 12 kg each weigh 48 kg in total. The charge is based on 64 kg.
