Document checklist

Which documents a shipment needs depends on the direction. The list only names what a source confirms. It does not cover import into China and India; please clarify those rules with your recipient.

For every shipment

  • Commercial invoice

    The commercial invoice is issued by the seller. The customs value of the goods is declared on its basis, for export as well as for import.

    Sources
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Notwendige Unterlagen, accessed 17 Nov 2026
    • IHK Pfalz: Ausfuhrdokumente, accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • Packing list

    If the shipment consists of several packages, the customs office can ask for a list of the packages.

    Source
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Notwendige Unterlagen, accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • Transport document

    Air waybill, bill of lading or another transport document. The customs office can ask for transport documents.

    Source
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Notwendige Unterlagen, accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • EORI number

    It is required for customs clearance and is stated in the customs declaration. Applications are free of charge through the German customs portal.

    Source
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): EORI-Nummer und Beantragung einer EORI-Nummer, accessed 16 Nov 2026

Export from Germany

  • Export declaration

    It is submitted electronically through ATLAS-Ausfuhr and receives a registration number (MRN). Small consignments up to EUR 1,000 can be declared orally. Details are in the customs FAQ.

    Sources
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Normales Verfahren (zweistufiges Ausfuhrverfahren), accessed 16 Nov 2026
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Warenausfuhr im einstufigen Verfahren (sogenannte Kleinsendungen), accessed 16 Nov 2026

  • Certificate of origin, if required

    The certificate of origin is a public document on the non-preferential origin of the goods. You apply for it at your chamber of commerce (IHK). Whether the Emirates or Saudi Arabia require it is stated in the sections below.

    Source
    • IHK für München und Oberbayern: Ursprungszeugnis, accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • Export control check

    Check whether your goods are listed as dual-use goods. If so, you need a licence from BAFA.

    Source
    • Bundesamt für Wirtschaft und Ausfuhrkontrolle (BAFA): Exportkontrolle und das BAFA (Leitfaden, Stand November 2025), accessed 16 Nov 2026

Import into Germany

  • Customs declaration

    The import must be declared to customs; the customs administration uses the IT system ATLAS for this. Details are in the customs FAQ.

    Source
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): ATLAS, accessed 18 Nov 2026

  • Tariff number of the goods

    The duty rate depends on it. It can be looked up in the electronic tariff (EZT-online) and in TARIC.

    Source
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Zollsatz (Einfuhr aus einem Nicht-EU-Staat), accessed 18 Nov 2026

  • Customs representative, if wanted

    If a freight forwarder or customs agent is to submit the declaration, appoint them as direct or indirect representative.

    Source
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Vertretung, accessed 18 Nov 2026

  • Proof of origin for tariff preferences, if applicable

    For a preferential duty rate, customs asks for the corresponding documents. Under the EU's Generalised Scheme of Preferences this is a statement on origin from an exporter registered in the REX system.

    Sources
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Notwendige Unterlagen, accessed 17 Nov 2026
    • Europäische Kommission, Generaldirektion Steuern und Zollunion: Generalised System of Preferences (GSP) (Abschnitt vom 13.10.2026), accessed 18 Nov 2026

  • Allow for import VAT

    19 percent, or 7 percent for goods listed in Annex 2 of the German VAT Act. Businesses can deduct it as input tax.

    Sources
    • Zoll (Generalzolldirektion): Zolltarifauskunft für Umsatzsteuerzwecke (Einfuhrumsatzsteuer), accessed 18 Nov 2026
    • Bundesministerium der Justiz, Gesetze im Internet: § 15 UStG: Vorsteuerabzug, accessed 18 Nov 2026

Import into the Emirates

  • Commercial invoice in Arabic or English

    The commercial invoice must be in Arabic or English. For German goods, the country of origin must be stated as “Federal Republic of Germany”. “European Union” is generally not sufficient.

    Source
    • Germany Trade & Invest (GTAI): Zollbericht Vereinigte Arabische Emirate: Zollanmeldung und Warenbegleitpapiere (Stand 28.07.2025), accessed 16 Nov 2026

  • Transport documents

    Commercial imports usually need a commercial invoice, transport documents and a packing list, and depending on the goods also permits.

    Source
    • Germany Trade & Invest (GTAI): Zollbericht Vereinigte Arabische Emirate: Zollanmeldung und Warenbegleitpapiere (Stand 28.07.2025), accessed 16 Nov 2026

  • Packing list

    It may be required in Arabic or English and with HS codes.

    Source
    • Germany Trade & Invest (GTAI): Zollbericht Vereinigte Arabische Emirate: Zollanmeldung und Warenbegleitpapiere (Stand 28.07.2025), accessed 16 Nov 2026

  • Attestation of the commercial invoice from AED 10,000

    For goods imported into the Emirates with a value of AED 10,000 or more, electronic attestation of the commercial invoice through the eDAS 2.0 system of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs is mandatory. It costs AED 150 per invoice plus service fees. Clarify with your recipient who carries out the attestation.

    Source
    • Außenministerium der VAE (MoFA): FAQs und Dienst „Attestation of commercial invoices via the Electronic Documents Attestation System (eDAS 2.0)“, accessed 16 Nov 2026

  • Certificate of origin

    Since 2020, a certificate of origin is generally no longer required in the Emirates.

    Source
    • Germany Trade & Invest (GTAI): Zollbericht Vereinigte Arabische Emirate: Zollanmeldung und Warenbegleitpapiere (Stand 28.07.2025), accessed 16 Nov 2026

  • Cosmetics for Dubai

    Cosmetic products must be registered in the Montaji system of Dubai Municipality before import and sale.

    Source
    • Dubai Municipality, Health & Safety Department: Technical Guidelines for Cosmetics and Personal Care Products, Version 2.1 (15.09.2025), accessed 16 Nov 2026

Import into Saudi Arabia

Whether the commercial invoice and certificate of origin for Saudi Arabia must be attested by a chamber of commerce or an embassy cannot be confirmed clearly. Please clarify this with your importer and your chamber of commerce.

  • Commercial invoice

    The Saudi customs authority ZATCA lists it as a document to be presented for import.

    Source
    • ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, Saudi-Arabien): Import Instructions (zuletzt aktualisiert 26.08.2026), accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • Bill of lading

    ZATCA lists the bill of lading as the transport document for import.

    Source
    • ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, Saudi-Arabien): Import Instructions (zuletzt aktualisiert 26.08.2026), accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • Certificate of origin

    ZATCA lists the certificate of origin as an import document. It can only be omitted if the country of origin is clear without it.

    Source
    • ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, Saudi-Arabien): Import Instructions (zuletzt aktualisiert 26.08.2026), accessed 17 Nov 2026

  • Conformity certificates through SABER

    The importer applies for the required conformity certificates on the SABER portal. The application must be made before the customs declaration, otherwise the goods cannot be imported.

    Sources
    • IHK Köln: Einfuhr Saudi-Arabien, accessed 16 Nov 2026
    • SASO (Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization): Saudi Standards Obligates Importers to Register All Products on Saber Platform (03.01.2021), accessed 16 Nov 2026

The checklist does not replace customs or legal advice. Requirements depend on the goods, value and recipient and can change.
