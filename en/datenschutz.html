No cookies, no tracking, no third-party content

The rate estimator works entirely in your browser. Your entries are not transmitted. When you carry the details over into the request form, this happens within your browser.

Request form and e-mail

When you make a request, your details are processed to answer the request and prepare an offer: company, contact person, contact details and the shipment details. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures at your request).

The form opens your own e-mail program with the finished request text. The request is sent as an e-mail through your e-mail provider.

Contact via WhatsApp

This website contains links that let you write to us on WhatsApp. No WhatsApp script is embedded: no data is transmitted to WhatsApp when you open the pages. Only when you click one of these links does your browser open a WhatsApp page or the WhatsApp app; from then on WhatsApp processes your data.

For users in the European Economic Area, the service is provided by WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland. According to WhatsApp, data is also transferred to countries outside the European Economic Area, including the USA. Details are in the WhatsApp privacy policy.

If you write via WhatsApp, the controller receives your phone number, your profile name and the content of your message and uses them to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your request aims at a contract, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests). You can object to processing under (f) at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).
